**Subject: Colony Party Store — licensee-built ordering software;
request for guidance**

Dear Michigan Liquor Control Commission,

My name is Antonios Kado. My family owns and operates Colony Party
Store, a licensed retailer at 6525 Dyke Road, Clay Township, Michigan
48001 (license #430342). I am writing to you both as part of a licensee
family and as the developer of the software our store now uses.

Over the past year I built Liquor Kings, a tool that helps our store
prepare and place its weekly spirits orders through the Commission's
Online Liquor Ordering system. It checks our order against the
Commission's published price book before anything is placed, the
order itself is placed under our own store account with the owner's
explicit authorization, and every confirmation number and total is
kept in a permanent record. Our store places its regular weekly order
with it today, and it has made our ordering more accurate — exact
quantities, current prices, complete records.

Other Michigan store owners have expressed interest in using it, and
before we take that step we want to do things the right way. That is
the purpose of this letter, and I would welcome the Commission's
guidance on three things:

1. Whether MLCC has a preferred or recommended path for software
   providers that assist licensees with OLO ordering — any
   registration, review, or technical guidance you offer or would
   like followed.
2. Best practices for the OLO sub-user system as the appropriate way
   for a licensee to authorize ordering assistance, which is the
   approach we intend to use.
3. Whether a brief call with the appropriate division would be
   possible, so we can walk through exactly how the software works
   and hear any concerns before we grow.

For context, other control states have begun working directly with
ordering platforms — Ohio's OHLQ announced an official integration
with a licensed-beverage ordering platform in 2025. We would be proud
to see Michigan licensees have the same kind of modern, compliant
tooling, built here in Michigan by a licensee family.

Our standing commitments: store credentials remain the licensee's
own and are stored encrypted; the software acts only with the
owner's explicit authorization; every order retains a complete audit
trail; and licensee data is never sold or shared.

Thank you for your time and for the work the Commission does. I can
be reached at (810) 671-3333 or tonykado30@gmail.com.

Respectfully,

Antonios Kado
Founder, Liquor Kings — on behalf of Colony Party Store, Clay Township, MI
